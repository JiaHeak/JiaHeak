## Hi there! 👋

I'm JiaHeak Tang, a Physics graduate from **Universiti Malaya (University of Malaya), Malaysia**, with a strong interest in **astrophysics, cosmology, and scientific computing, and AI/ML**. I am preparing to pursue postgraduate research in astrophysics, with a particular focus on galaxy formation and evolution.

My research journey began in **observational radio astronomy**, where I investigated the multiwavelength variability and physical properties of the blazar **PKS 1510−089** using radio and gamma-ray observations. Since then, I have expanded my research experience into **computational cosmology**, including cosmological simulations, large-scale structure reconstruction, and numerical methods for studying galaxy evolution.

Currently, my research interests centre on **galaxy evolution, baryonic physics, and the circumgalactic medium (CGM)**. I am particularly interested in understanding the baryon cycle—how gas accretion, feedback, and outflows regulate galaxy evolution—and connecting theoretical simulations with astronomical observations.

I'm always interested in exploring new computational methods, collaborating on research projects, and developing open-source tools for the astronomy community. 🌌
